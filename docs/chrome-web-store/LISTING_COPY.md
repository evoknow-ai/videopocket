## Detailed description

VideoPocket helps Mac users save social-media videos and images they created, own, or have permission to download.

Supported media:

- X/Twitter videos
- Facebook videos and Reels
- Instagram videos and Reels
- Instagram post and carousel images

VideoPocket places a simple Save control on supported media. Direct files use Chrome's download manager. When a platform provides separate or segmented audio and video streams, the signed VideoPocket Mac Helper merges and converts them locally into a QuickTime-compatible H.264/AAC MP4.

Downloads remain organized under `~/Downloads/VideoPocket`, with separate folders for X, Facebook, and Instagram. The extension popup shows live progress through downloading, merging, converting, and ready stages, and includes quick access to downloads, recent errors, updates, and the changelog.

Privacy is built into the architecture:

- No VideoPocket cloud service
- No analytics or advertising trackers
- No account required
- No browser-cookie export
- No media uploaded to EVOKNOW, Inc.
- Local Helper communication is limited to `127.0.0.1`

The separately installed Mac Helper is required for media that cannot be downloaded directly. It is signed, notarized, and available from the official VideoPocket GitHub Release.

Only download content you created, own, or are authorized to save. VideoPocket does not grant rights to third-party content, bypass DRM, or support YouTube.
